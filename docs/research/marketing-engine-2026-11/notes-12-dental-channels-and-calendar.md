## Open Dental

### Takeaway
Open Dental's Third-Party Vendors page now lists NexHealth as explicitly unpartnered with a 2026 warning, which directly affects any PMS path that relies on NexHealth for Open Dental practices.

### Cited Findings
- Listing: only vendors with a verified API status; request or dispute at vendor.relations@opendental.com; statuses Authorized, Authorized in Transition (needs active contract and timeline), Dangerous/Unknown, Not Currently Active (no activity in 60 days); no fees mentioned; API access via apisetup.html. FACT. — [OD Third-Party Vendors](https://www.opendental.com/site/vendorsthirdparty.html)
- "Dangerous/Unknown" text: direct database writing is dangerous; "should be avoided as they can harm your data, including patient data, and are not guaranteed to work as Open Dental releases new versions." FACT.
- Warnings, verbatim from the page: 06/05/2026 NexHealth: "NexHealth is not partnered with or associated with Open Dental in any way."; 08/08/2024 NexHealth: "We specifically advise customers not to use NexHealth products that claim to integrate with Open Dental."; 07/26/2024 Adit: "Open Dental is advising practices not to use Adit."; Modento (10/24/2024), Retrace (02/22/2024), Weave (01/18/2024): concerns "addressed and resolved." The old thirdpartywarnings.html page redirects to this page. FACT.
