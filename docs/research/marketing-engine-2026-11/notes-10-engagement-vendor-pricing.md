## 1. Per-vendor table: pricing, terms, signup model (self-serve vs demo-gated)

## 2. Cloud practice-management systems that bundle engagement

### Takeaway
Cloud PMS vendors increasingly publish prices (Archy $899, CareStack from $829, Oryx $650-$899 with a $0 startup tier), and all bundle texting/forms/booking, but their prices are 3-4x DreamCRM's and require a PMS switch, so they are an alternative for practices already leaving their PMS, not a like-for-like substitute.

### Cited Findings
- Archy — FACT (2026-11-23): "Run your practice on Archy for $899/month" per location; unlimited users, providers, claims, eligibility checks, texting; new-practice offer $400/month until 400 patients or 1 year; CTA "Get pricing" / "Get a Demo". Raised $50M Series C (Becker's snippet: "Sept. 11 news release"; the Archy page itself shows "We raised a $50M Series C", consistent with Sept 2026; earlier rounds: Series B $20M Oct 30, Series A $15M). — [Archy pricing](https://www.archy.com/pricing), [Becker's search: Archy](https://www.beckersdental.com/?s=archy)
- CareStack — FACT: bundles Essentials "Starting at $829 / month" (scheduling, online appointments, charting, imaging, patient portal and forms, eligibility, claims, email/MMS/bulk texting) and Intelligence "Starting at $1299 / month", more tiers and add-ons; CTA "Book a Demo"; FAQ says an implementation fee may be charged "in certain cases"; "Trusted by 3000+ Dental Practices". Becker's snippets (undated): Overjet and CareStack "Smart Dental Platform"; Pearl AI integration; Straumann minority stake and investment. — [CareStack pricing](https://www.carestack.com/pricing), [Becker's search](https://www.beckersdental.com/?s=carestack)
- Oryx — FACT: US pricing: Startup Offer Oryx Pro & Automate $0/mo (regular price starts at 200 patients or 12 months, setup fee $1) and Oryx AI $400/mo; Oryx Pro $650/mo (payments start 3 months after signup, up to 2 providers, +$100/mo each additional); Oryx Automate $899/mo; includes patient engagement, online forms, two-way text, reminders, portal, online scheduling, eligibility; integrates Mango VoIP ("contract through Mango"); CTA "Book a Demo". ## 4. Contract terms practices complain about

### Takeaway
I have vendor-stated terms but no retrieved customer complaints in this pass. Documented clauses of note: Weave's 10%/12-month unilateral increase and 30-day non-renewal window on 1-year+ orders; NexHealth's 90-day non-renewal notice on annual plans; Solutionreach's yearly contracts and right to charge for features at any time.

### Inferences
- A "no contract, cancel anytime" claim is a real differentiator against Weave, Solutionreach and NexHealth annual plans, but not against Flex.
