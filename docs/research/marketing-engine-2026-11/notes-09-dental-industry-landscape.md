## (a) Competitor moves, 2026

### Takeaway
The single biggest change since September: Weave was taken private by Francisco Partners (announced Aug 18, closed ~Oct 27, 2026), with an explicit AI/payments push and no stated pricing changes. No self-serve entrant at or below ~$300/mo with real PMS sync was found; one lightweight self-serve texting tool (Sociocs) exists but does not integrate with practice-management software.

## (b) Practice economics and buyer state

### Takeaway
Practices are busier but squeezed: reimbursement trails inflation, hiring is hard, and dentist confidence fell to a multi-year low in Q3 2026. DSO affiliation keeps rising (16-17%), so the independent buyer base is shrinking slowly.

### Cited Findings
- FACT: Starting Q1 2026 HPI publishes dentist-panel results only via quarterly State of the U.S. Dental Economy reports; Q3 2026 update is out with a special focus on staff recruiting. — [ADA HPI](https://www.ada.org/resources/research/health-policy-institute/dental-care-market/state-of-the-us-dental-economy)
- Q3 2026 (secondary, Becker's snippets): confidence dropped to 26% (headline "Dentists' confidence in the economy drops to 26% in Q3", [link](https://www.beckersdental.com/?p=27599)); 750 respondents; 21.2% had hygienist roles open >6 months ([Becker's](https://www.beckersdental.com/?p=27753)); 42.3% planned to add staff, 40.7% did ([Becker's](https://www.beckersdental.com/?p=27524)). Schedule-fullness and fee figures not retrieved.
- Q2 2026: practices busier, reimbursement still behind inflation. — [Dentaltown](https://www.dentaltown.com/news/details/15076/dental-practices-reported-fuller-schedules-as-reimbursement-trailed-inflation)
- Q1 2026: one-third of dentists said not busy enough; Q4 2025 panel: staffing top 2026 concern (54.2%), volume/schedule 31.8%. — [ADA News Huddles](https://adanews.ada.org/huddles/dental-fiscal-squeeze-continues-into-2026/)
- Aug 2026: HPI says practice expenses outpace revenue for general-dentist owners. — [ADA News](https://adanews.ada.org/new-dentist/2026/august/hpi-dental-practice-expenses-outpace-revenue-for-general-dentist-owners/)
- DSO: affiliated dentists 17% in 2025 (from 7.2% in 2015); solo share 32.6% (2025) from 36.5% (2022); TUSK Q4 2026 says 16.1% and practice ownership at a 20-year low of 73%. — [Becker's](https://www.beckersdental.com/?p=27683), [Dentistry Today/TUSK](https://www.dentistrytoday.com/tusk-practice-sales-releases-q4-2026-dental-market-report/)
- 69% of DSOs planned more acquisitions in 2026 (VENDOR/survey). — [Becker's](https://www.beckersdental.com/?p=23630)

### Inferences
- Triggers: front-desk understaffing (a tool that "does the job" fits), margin squeeze (price sensitivity favors month-to-month $200), and year-end/Jan benefit-reset phone volume.

### Gaps
- Not found: number of US practices, front-desk wage/turnover figures, online scheduling/AI/texting adoption stats, membership-plan adoption, dropping-insurance trend, 2027 software purchase intent.
