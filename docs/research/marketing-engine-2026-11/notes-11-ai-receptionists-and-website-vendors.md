# AI receptionists and dental website/marketing vendors (US, as of 2026-11-23)

Method note: ~18 tool calls. WebSearch returns summarized snippets (not raw pages); several primary pages (DrBicuspid, Dentaltown) returned 403 or JS walls. Most price data is THIRD-PARTY (often a competing vendor's blog). Labels: FACT = vendor's own page (fetched), THIRD-PARTY = other source, ESTIMATE = my inference.

## Group A: AI receptionist vendors (who, price, terms, claims, evidence)

### Takeaway
Most AI-receptionist vendors do not publish prices. Third-party roundups put the category at roughly $299-$1,500/location/month, and nearly all sell via demo or quote. Only Viva, Dentina and Weave's base plan have price points visible. Independent evidence on performance is thin; most "review" content is competitor-vendor blogging.

### Cited Findings
- Viva AI: "starts at $350/month on the current promotional plan"; DSO (10+ locations) custom; "SOC 2 Type II + HIPAA"; "100+ languages"; "Direct write-back to Dentrix Ascend, CareStack, Cloud9, and others"; outbound recall/reactivation "in production"; "Schedule a live demo" (no self-serve shown); contract term, funding, customer count not stated on page. FACT (vendor page, fetched 2026-11-23) — [Viva](https://www.getviva.ai/ai-receptionist-software/)
- Arini: no published prices (demo-based, per location/agents). Y Combinator W24; reported seed ~$500K, total ~$630K (trackers disagree; Crunchbase page unreliable). A listing shows "starting $499" (unconfirmed) — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10036210/Arini/). ## Size of the AI-receptionist trend, practice sentiment, and rules

### Takeaway
General dentist AI use is substantial (43%), but front-desk check-in AI is only ~10%; no independent receptionist-specific adoption figure found. Compliance guidance is mostly vendor/law-firm blogs; no dental-specific federal rule.

### Cited Findings
- ADA HPI (published July 2026): 43.3% of dentists use AI for at least one task, 26.4% plan to; front desk check-in 10.1%, insurance verification 13.6%, social media 10.7%, imaging 22.8%; 32.4% report being overworked; concerns: replacing human judgment, errors. FACT (ADA page) — [ADA HPI](https://www.ada.org/resources/research/health-policy-institute/dental-practice-research/dentists-ai-usage-and-attitudes)
- Other surveys: ~32% use AI, 38% considering (Becker's); 30% plan never to use AI — [Becker's](https://www.beckersdental.com/dentists/30-of-dentists-plan-to-never-use-ai-5-things-to-know-about-dentists-ai-integration/); Zentist RCM report: 58% adopted/plan AI in 2026 (vendor report, via search summary).
- Trade coverage exists: DrBicuspid "Is your AI dental receptionist failing?" (not readable, 403), Dental Economics "AI dental receptionists: hype, reality" — [DrBicuspid](https://www.drbicuspid.com/clinical/treatment-software/ai/article/15774740/is-your-ai-dental-receptionist-failing), [Dental Economics](https://www.dentaleconomics.com/science-tech/artificial-intelligence/article/55291712/ai-dental-receptionists-hype-reality-and-how-to-build-one-yourself)
- Foji (June 2026 vendor blog): voice receptionists are the most common pitch, but best ROI is in insurance/billing — [Foji](https://www.foji.io/blog/ai-employees-for-dental-offices-what-actually-works-in-2026)
- Rules (all THIRD-PARTY, vendor/law blogs; verify with counsel): all-party recording consent in ~13 states incl. California (Penal Code 632); California bot-disclosure law; Maine (2025) AI disclosure; Utah on request; Colorado AI Act likely not "high-risk" for receptionists; Louisiana Act 649 covers AI-assisted clinical recording disclosure (not phone agents); HIPAA BAA is the main weight — [JustCall](https://justcall.io/blog/is-ai-receptionist-hipaa-compliant.html), [Enzuzo](https://www.enzuzo.com/blog/ai-voice-agent-compliance), [AGG on Louisiana](https://www.agg.com/news-insights/publications/louisiana-requires-verbal-disclosure-before-ai-assisted-recording-of-patient-encounters/)

### Inferences
- A text/web-based AI that drafts for approval (no voice) avoids most recording-consent exposure. ESTIMATE.

### Gaps
- No independent 2026 adoption % for AI receptionists; no verbatim Reddit/Dentaltown/Facebook sentiment retrieved; conference buzz (e.g. Yankee, ADA SmileCon) not sourced.

## "AI employee / AI team member" positioning

### Takeaway
The phrase is widely used by small vendors and agencies as a generic framing, usually "augments the front desk, staff review exceptions"; Weave uses "always-on teammate", not "AI employee".

### Cited Findings
- LowCode Agency, Foji and others sell "AI employees for dental practices" defined as software handling scheduling, intake, reminders, insurance verification with staff reviewing exceptions; one says treat it like a new hire with its own permissions. Claims (25% call-volume drop, ROI in 60-90 days) are unverified vendor claims — [LowCode](https://www.lowcode.agency/blog/ai-employee-for-dental-practices), [Foji](https://www.foji.io/blog/ai-employees-for-dental-offices-what-actually-works-in-2026)
- Weave: "Your always-on teammate" (FACT, above). Oral Health Group, "Beyond the AI receptionist: agentic AI in the dental front office" — [OHG](https://www.oralhealthgroup.com/features/beyond-the-ai-receptionist-agentic-ai-in-the-dental-front-office/)
- DocSites (website vendor) now advertises AI webchat, AI phone answering and AI scheduler — [DocSites](https://www.docsites.com/about/)

### Inferences
- "AI employee" is not ownable; differentiation must come from approval-gated actions and a named ledger, not the phrase. ESTIMATE.

### Gaps
- Didn't verify Dental Intelligence or Henry Schein One positioning.

## Group B: website and marketing vendors

### Takeaway
Template agencies charge ~$2-8K setup plus ~$100-400/mo and, per competitors, usually keep the site on exit. Full marketing programs run thousands per month. Vendors rarely publish price or terms.

### Cited Findings
- Template agencies (ProSites, PBHS, Smile Marketing, Officite): $2,000-$8,000 upfront, $100-$400/mo; SEO may be +$300/mo; "if you cancel, you usually lose the site itself"; some bill full annual amount on cancel. THIRD-PARTY and competitor-authored — [DentalBase](https://www.dentalbase.ai/blogs/dental-website-cost)
- TDO (competitor): ProSites uses a proprietary CMS, PBHS ties site to its infrastructure — [TDO](https://wwww.tdo4endo.com/website-design/)
- ADA advises owning domain, content and code, and having an attorney review contracts — [ADA](https://www.ada.org/resources/practice/practice-management/25_websitedevelopment_considerationswhenoutsourcing)
- ProSites pricing "per feature"/not public; Officite "contact vendor"; one Capterra reviewer cites $90/mo hosting — [Capterra ProSites](https://www.capterra.com/p/217023/ProSites/), [Capterra Officite](https://www.capterra.com/p/231739/Officite/reviews/)
- Roadside Dental Marketing: monthly campaign listed at $1,500/mo (FACT, per search summary) — [Roadside](https://www.roadsidedentalmarketing.com/product-pricing/)
- Agency guide: healthy single-location program $4,500-$10,000/mo including ad spend, 6-month commitment standard — [Redefine](https://redefineweb.com/blog/dental-marketing-agency-pricing-costly-red-flags/)
- Weave starts at $199/mo plus $149 bundles (FACT above) is the nearest communications comparator.

### Inferences
- DreamCRM's $200 all-in is below even the cheapest template tier plus a patient-comms tool (~$300-$550/mo). ESTIMATE from above figures.

### Gaps
- Not found: Sesame, Wonderist, My Social Practice, Delmain, Gargle, Scorpion, Tebra, Great Dental Websites, Smile Savvy fees/terms; Wix/Squarespace/GoDaddy dental usage; Reddit/Dentaltown complaint threads; PBHS contract terms.

## Typical all-in spend and AI-search (GEO) services

### Takeaway
No audited average exists; benchmarks are percent-of-collections from agency blogs. GEO is sold mostly by custom quote; demand evidence is vendor-produced.

### Cited Findings
- Marketing budget guidance: ~2-5% to 4-7% of collections for established practices; ~$2,500/mo minimum for measurable results; ADA HPI survey does not break out marketing. THIRD-PARTY — [Bulletproof Dental](https://www.bulletproofdentalpractice.com/dental-marketing-budget-percentage/), [DentalBase](https://www.dentalbase.ai/blogs/dental-marketing-budget)
- Dental SEO: $500/mo "usually a poor investment" vs $2,500-$6,000/mo with a specialist (Winston, agency opinion); GEO listed by agencies mostly "custom pricing" (First Page Sage from $10,000/project; one agency from $1,600/mo) — [Winston](https://winstondigitalmarketing.com/playbooks/dental-seo-pricing/), [WebTonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/best-geo-gen-engine-opt-agencies-for-dental)
- Demand evidence: Rater8 2025 survey (26% say AI tools influenced provider choice; original not found; healthcare, not dental-only); Birdeye 2026 AI Search Readiness Report: Dental; Dentaltown/DentistryIQ pieces warn of zero-click shifts. One article concedes few patients use ChatGPT for dentists today — [Birdeye](https://birdeye.com/uk/resources/guides/the-2026-ai-search-readiness-report-dental/), [Dentaltown](https://www.dentaltown.com/channel/post/26365/are-patients-really-using-chatgpt-to-find-dentists-what-the-data-actually-shows)
- 55% walked away from a provider over online reviews (Rater8 via secondary article) — [DentistryIQ](https://dentistryiq.com/business-management-leadership/practice-growth-marketing/news/55383959/how-to-attract-new-patients-in-the-age-of-ai-searchas-ai-tools-increasingly-shape-how-patients-evaluate-healthcare-providers-your-online-reputation-may-influence-patient-decisions-before-they-ever-visit-your-website-or-call-your-office-new-survey-data-reveal-that-online-reviews-social-media-content-and-google-business-profile-activity-now-play-a-growing-role-in-both-ai-generated-recommendations-and-patient-trustmaking-proactive-reputation-management-a-critical-practice-growth-strategy)

### Inferences
- Honest positioning: reviews, GBP and clean structured site data feed AI answers, but no vendor can guarantee AI citations. ESTIMATE.

### Gaps
- No dated survey of actual all-in monthly spend on website+SEO+reputation+comms; no hard evidence of dentist purchase demand for GEO.
