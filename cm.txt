DREAMCRM-116: the clinic-site dimmed-text slice (UI batch 72)

Closes punch-list entry 4. `components/clinic-site` was the last tree
excluded from `tests/a11y/dimmed-text.test.ts`; it is in scope now, and
nothing in it is pardoned.

THE DEFERRAL'S STATED REASON WAS A MEASUREMENT, so the measurement is what
discharges it. The tree's ink and ground are derived per tenant, so the rule
is graded across every brand a clinic can actually pick — the twelve
`tests/clinic-site/palette.test.ts` pins its AA floor over — through
`buildClinicPalette` rather than one clinic's value. The answer does not
depend on the brand, which is the finding:

  - the tenant body ink at 50% measures 3.20-3.29 against a 4.5 floor. The
    BEST case fails, and the spread across twelve brands is 0.09, because
    the builder grades `ink` on `bg` with very little headroom and halving
    it spends all of that whatever the hue;
  - the named quiet ink measures 4.88-5.97, so this rule's standing fix is
    available for every brand rather than the lucky ones;
  - the cosmetic template's hover arrow was under the GRAPHIC floor, not
    just the type one: its accent at 30% on cream measures 1.46-1.97
    against the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.11 asks. At full strength that accent never
    measures below 4.62, by construction.

THE ENTRY'S COUNT WAS WRONG IN BOTH HALVES. Seven chunks carry an
`opacity-N`, six unprefixed — not three. Four are graphics with no type
scale and the rule leaves them alone as it leaves the other 22; three
declared their own type scale and all three are fixed.

AND WIDENING THE DIRECTORY DID NOT FINISH SWEEPING IT. Every rule in this
repo reads quoted CLASS strings, so `style={{ color: INK_MUTED, opacity:
0.65 }}` is invisible to all of them. Two live defects sat in this tree that
way and were found by hand — the Google rating badge's "· N reviews on
Google" at 2.66 on the light ground (public body copy on the home and
services pages) and a team placeholder at 2.54-2.83. Both fixed. The missing
READER is new punch-list entry 7, with its population measured (24 inline
non-endpoint opacities tree-wide, 4 on a declared ink or type) so it is a
bounded job rather than a rumour.
